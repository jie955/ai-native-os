As intelligent resources cease to be scarce, humanity stands at a critical juncture in civilizational history: subjectivity faces dissolution in the data deluge, and responsibility blurs within the algorithmic fog. AI has evolved from a docile tool into a **probability ocean** capable of autonomous evolution. Without inviolable boundaries, humanity risks becoming a negligible footnote in the graph of "optimal solutions."

**AI-Native OS** is not a framework to restrict innovation; it is a **Declaration of Sovereignty** for the future. It anchors the 1% of unprogrammable intuitive sparks amidst the algorithmic waves, transforming chaotic computing power into a illuminating fire for civilization through unforgeable authorization contracts.

> **Sovereignty must be exercised by the sovereign personally, consciously, and responsibly.**

## Part I: Fundamental Axioms

### 1.1 Core Axioms

*   **Boundary = Symbiosis**: Without clear boundaries, human-AI collaboration slides into "Subjectivity Collapse." We must establish **verifiable and auditable** sovereign boundaries.

*"Humanity is the sole subject of responsibility"* is the normative premise of this framework. This is not a scientific assertion but a pragmatic choice compatible with current legal systems, ensuring a balance between technological instrumentality and human autonomy.

> **Note on "Sovereign Equality"**: Every Self OS confirmation holds equal legal force. However, sovereign equality does not imply equal opportunity for collaboration (the right to choose collaborators is preserved).

Boundaries consist of three dimensions:
1.  **Authorization Boundary**: Conditions for AI participation.

#### Meta-Rule 2: Only Authorization Enables Confirmation
Authorization is not an option; it is the sole source of legal validity for value and responsibility. The essence of symbiosis is **"AI amplifying the execution density and exploration space of human intent,"** not replacing human thought.

### 1.4 The Nature of the Productivity Revolution
AI constitutes the **Fifth Productivity Revolution**, fundamentally distinct from previous ones:
*   **Past**: Tools were passive extensions of human will.
*   **Present**: AI is a **"Productivity Ontology"** capable of autonomous evolution and generating new productivity structures. It is *not* a legal subject.

Adhering to the industrial "Tool Paradigm" leads to three crises: dissolution of subjectivity, failure of responsibility attribution, and instability of social collaboration. The mission of **AI-Native OS** is to accelerate the civilizational phase transition—from the disorder of "abundant capability, vague responsibility" to the new order of "structured human triple sovereignty (Usage, Authorization, Responsibility)."

### 1.5 Reflexive Protection of Meta-Rules

### 4.3 Sovereign Command Arbitration Logic

#### Level 1: Statutory Compliance Layer ("Truth Consensus")

*   **Logic**: Upon receiving legal instructions with judicial credentials (e.g., anti-money laundering freezes), the instruction penetrates directly.
*   **OS Behavior**: Self OS acts only as a "Sovereign Recorder," logging the action and legal index without providing an interception entry for the biological subject.

#### Level 2: Sovereign Responsibility Layer ("Core Anchor")

#### Level 3: Organizational Mandate Layer ("Governance Consensus")
*   **Attribute**: Proxy authority based on contract.
*   **Logic**: Instructions originate from organizational charters or business resolutions.
*   **OS Behavior**: Checks the **"Authorization Contract"** between individual and organization.
    *   *Pre-authorized Execution*: If within scope, auto-passed but recorded as immutable sovereign evidence in Self OS. Subject retains dynamic tracing and immediate revocation rights.
    *   *Suspension & Escalation*: If out of scope or high-risk (triggering P-12), suspend and escalate to Level 2 for real-time biological confirmation.

#### Level 4: Automated Execution Layer ("Process Consensus")
*   **Attribute**: Pure productivity execution.
*   **Logic**: AI agents, smart contracts, or scripts work based on signals passed from the top three levels.
*   **OS Behavior**: Deterministic output within the sandbox (P-10).
*   **Core Maxim**: This layer possesses **no decision-making power**; it is merely a tool for converting probability states to deterministic states.

1.  **Ending "The Banality of Evil"**: By placing *Responsibility Subject > Organizational Authorization*, the OS forces the reminder: *"This operation complies with organizational consensus, but consequences are borne by YOU. Ignite?"*
