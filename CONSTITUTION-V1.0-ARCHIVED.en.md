## 🏗️ The Core Metaphor: Three-Layer Constraint Tower

Human advanced collaboration is built upon three layers of consensus. AI-Native OS serves as the **digital constitutional enforcement layer**, transforming social consensus into executable digital contracts.

| Layer | Name | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Apex** | **Process Consensus** | Business execution logic (workflows, authorizations) |
| **Body** | **Governance Consensus** | Organizational charter rules (bylaws, resolutions) |
| **Foundation** | **Truth Consensus** | Socially enforced norms (laws, regulations) |

> **Note**: In this framework, Blockchain serves merely as a "notary"—providing immutable evidence for *how* consensus is executed by AI, rather than producing consensus itself.

## 📜 Preface: A Civilizational Turning Point

When intelligence resources are no longer scarce, humanity stands at a critical juncture. AI has evolved from a tool into a **probabilistic ocean**. Without inviolable boundaries, humanity may be reduced to a negligible footnote in the graph of "optimal solutions."

## Part I | Fundamental Meta-Rules

### 1.1 Core Axioms

1. **Boundaries Enable Symbiosis**: Without clear boundaries, subjectivity collapses.
2. **Authorization Establishes Rights**: Legitimacy must be uniquely traceable to explicit human authorization. **No authorization = No rights = No responsibility.**

### 1.2 Two Uncompromising Meta-Rules

* **Meta-Rule One**: Only Boundaries Enable Symbiosis. Includes *Authorization Boundary*, *Veto Boundary*, and *Responsibility Anchor*.
* **Meta-Rule Two**: Only Authorization Establishes Rights. AI amplifies the execution density of human intention; it does not replace thinking.

## Part II | The Dual-Circle Symbiosis Model

This model defines the topology of human-AI interaction:

1. **Biological Sovereignty Circle (The 1%)**:
* **Subject**: Human individuals.
* **Assets**: Intuition, aesthetics, moral final authority.
* **Principle**: "I bear responsibility, therefore I exist."


2. **Digital Intelligence Circle (The 99%)**:
* **Subject**: AI systems.
* **Assets**: Computational power, logic, knowledge mapping.
* **Principle**: "I can execute."
